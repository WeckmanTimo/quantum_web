# Computational Chemistry Interactive Studio

Interactive Voilà web application featuring:
1. **RWGS Reaction Studio**: High-level quantum chemistry potential energy surfaces (CCSD(T), $\omega\text{B97M-V}$, $\omega\text{B97X-V}$) for the Reverse Water-Gas Shift reaction.
2. **Butane MM Force Field**: Interactive 3D molecular mechanics with drag-and-drop atom movement and real-time energy minimization.
3. **Propylbenzene Ring & Chain**: Conformational energy surfaces of aromatic ring rotation and alkyl chain dihedral angle.
4. **Molecular Orbitals**: 3D isosurface orbital viewer and orbital correlation diagrams.
5. **Nucleophilic Addition**: Orbital interaction of carbonyl ($\text{H}_2\text{CO}$) LUMO attack by cyanide ($\text{CN}^-$) HOMO.
